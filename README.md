The World Is Grateful for You

A small interactive art experience by C.D. Good.

Where it began

Some projects begin with a plan. This one began with a day.

After a period of reflection, a walk, an autumn wreath and a return to watercolour, eighteen small blessing cards began to take shape.

The paintings were made by hand. The words grew from reflections on beauty, attention, generosity, beginnings and the things that make us human.

The idea of bringing them into a small interactive website followed naturally.

You can read the story of that day here:

The Pause Before the Picture — C.D. Good on Patreon

The experience

The World Is Grateful for You is an invitation to pause.

Draw a card, turn it over and receive a reflection. Explore the collection or print the eighteen cards to share with others.

The digital experience extends the original paintings into another form of encounter.

It is a small offering, made with care, and freely shared.

Technology

Built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with AI-assisted creative and technical collaboration.

Hosted as a static website through Netlify, with source files maintained on GitHub.
